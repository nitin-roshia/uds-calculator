Part C — villa unit count
Logic: Two independent constraints. The smaller result is your design unit count.
Footprint limit
Max ground coverage area (40% of net plot) ÷ each villa's ground floor footprint. How many villas fit physically on the ground. Primary constraint for luxury villas.
BAR ceiling
Net saleable BAR ÷ total villa BUA (all floors). Regulatory upper limit from FAR. For luxury villas this is usually higher than the footprint limit.
UDS plot share
Net plot ÷ final unit count. Each buyer's undivided land share — use for garden sizing and layout. Not a legal sub-division.
