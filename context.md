WHAT UDS MEANS FOR THIS PROJECT
--------------------------------
In Indian group housing law, individual plots cannot be demarcated or sold. Instead,
each buyer receives:
  (a) An undivided share of the total land area, proportional to their villa's
      built-up area as a fraction of total project built-up area (the "UDS").
  (b) Exclusive possession of their specific villa unit under a possession agreement.
  (c) Membership of the Residents Welfare Association (RWA), which takes over the
      common amenity area and green spaces upon project completion.
The UDS plot share per villa (net plot ÷ unit count) is used by the architect as a
design reference to size private gardens and confirm layout — it is not a legal
sub-division of the land.

UNIT COUNT LOGIC (FOOTPRINT-FIRST)
------------------------------------
The binding constraint for luxury villas is ground coverage (footprint), not FAR.
Correct method:
  Step 1: Net plot area
  Step 2: Deduct setbacks → reduces available footprint
  Step 3: Deduct internal roads → further reduces footprint
  Step 4: Deduct amenity block footprint → further reduces
  Step 5: Remaining = net villa footprint area
  Step 6: Divide by each villa's ground floor area → unit count (Row 1 / primary)
  Step 7: Cross-check against saleable BAR ÷ total villa BUA → BAR ceiling (Row 2)
  Step 8: Smaller of Row 1 and Row 2 = final design unit count

At 400 sq.m. BUA per villa with 50% ground floor ratio (200 sq.m. G/F footprint):
  Net villa footprint ~312 sq.m. → approximately 1–2 villas on ground floor alone.
  This confirms G+1 duplex format is essential. At G+1, each footprint yields one
  villa of ~400 sq.m. The footprint count doubles in effective unit count.
  Realistic luxury villa count: 8–14 units depending on final layout, road alignment,
  and green area placement.

BAR CHECK: 9,817 sq.m. saleable BAR ÷ 400 sq.m. per villa = 24 units maximum by FAR.
FAR is NOT the binding limit — footprint is. The two numbers must both be satisfied.

MANDATORY NON-NEGOTIABLES (KEY ONES)
--------------------------------------
- Silver IGBC / GRIHA 3★ green certification: MANDATORY because total BAR exceeds
  10,000 sq.m. (MBB Bylaw 4.15(v)). Must be factored into project cost from day one.
  UPSIDE: Silver certification unlocks +4% incentive BAR free (MBB Bylaw 4.15(ii))
  = ~405 sq.m. additional saleable area at no extra land cost.
- 2–5% of total BAR as non-saleable amenity area (club, gym, pool, community hall,
  RWA office, etc.) — transferred to RWA on completion (MBB Bylaw 5(vi)).
- Min 1% of total BAR as daily-use retail shops, max 45 sq.m. per shop (Bylaw 5(vii)).
- 15% of net plot as green area; 50% of that at natural ground level (Bylaw 8.1).
- Rainwater harvesting (Bylaw 8.3), rooftop solar in common areas (Bylaw 8.4).
- 2 trees per 80 sq.m. of plot area (Bylaw 8.2).
